TEDDYSTEPS · CHARACTER SYSTEM V3

Meet the gang.

Four animals, made like hand-cut stickers: one round shape, one bold pattern, crayon grain and wobbly edges, and the same sweet face. They're never just standing there. They bob, sway, drift and hop through the app, so even in the background the page feels alive.

THE LITTLE ONE
Bo the bear
Big hugs, bigger naps. Stands in for your baby.
BRAVE FIRSTS
Tiggy the tiger
Rolling, crawling, first steps: Tiggy goes first.
BUSY DAYS
Bizzy the bee
First foods, first words, first everything. Never sits still.
COSY THINGS
Puddle the duck
Bath time, bedtime and every splash in between.
HOW THEY SHOW UP
Hero
Big, front and centre, dancing. Welcome screens, celebrations.
Peek
Cropped by the edge of a card or header, as if they wandered in.
Pattern
Small and scattered behind content, like wallpaper that breathes.
FIRST WORD!
Sidekick
Tiny, next to a line of text or inside a badge, nodding along.
HOW WE MAKE THEM
01
One round shape
Every animal starts as a fat, soft circle-ish body, like a sticker cut with blunt scissors.
02
Crayon grain, wobbly edges
A paper texture and a slightly uneven outline make them feel printed and handmade, never vector-perfect.
03
One face for all
Shiny dot eyes, a tiny smile, big rose cheeks. Happy eyes when they celebrate.
04
One pattern each
Stripes for Tiggy and Bizzy, a muzzle for Bo, wings and a beak for Puddle. Nothing more.
DO
Let them overlap cards, photos and edges. Keep them moving, slowly and out of sync. Max two heroes per screen; peeks and patterns are free.
DON'T
Don't add outlines, gradients, mouths with teeth or new animals. Don't put them on top of text people need to read.
