TEDDYSTEPS · MVP SCOPE · 29 OF 52 SCREENS

Launch the loop, not the whole trail.

The first version only has to prove one loop: a parent pins a moment, family sends love within a day, and the parent comes back next week. Everything that serves that loop ships at launch. The book is the business, but nobody needs checkout until their trail turns one, so it follows in v1.1.
