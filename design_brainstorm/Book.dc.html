Confessions from year one

Things nobody tells you about year one

Every new parent finds these out the hard way. BabySteps was built around all three.

You'll take a truly unreasonable number of photos.
Mostly of the same nap.
BabySteps pins the best one to its week, so the rest of the camera roll can relax.
You'll remember almost none of it.
Not the first giggle. Not the first bath. (Nobody does.)
Every first lands on the trail the moment it happens, so the blur gets a map.
Your phone will be the only one who knows what day it is.
Tuesday? Week 14? Does it even matter?
BabySteps keeps count of the weeks, so you can just keep holding them.
